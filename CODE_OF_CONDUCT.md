# Code of Conduct

## Scope and expectations

This code applies to this project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces. It applies to contributors, users, and maintainers.
People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Offer specific,
constructive feedback, respect disagreement, credit others' work, and accept
reasonable requests to stop disruptive behavior. Treat newcomers respectfully.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation,
spam, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without consent,
and retaliation against someone raising a concern are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Do not post private evidence, personal information, or sensitive conduct reports
in a public issue. For abusive content or behavior on GitHub, use
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Where available, choose reporting to repository admins; otherwise use the GitHub
Support reporting option. GitHub handles reports sent to GitHub Support under
its own policies, not this project's moderation process.

A dedicated private maintainer contact has not yet been designated. This policy
will be updated when one is provided. Security vulnerabilities belong under the
separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may request a correction, remove inappropriate content, lock a
conversation, or restrict participation based on severity and repeated behavior.
Serious threats or harassment can lead to immediate restrictions. Moderation
should be proportionate and based on behavior, not a person's identity or
technical disagreement.

When a maintainer reviews a concern, they should consider available evidence,
protect sensitive information, and avoid disclosing a reporter's identity without
permission except where required for safety or by law. A maintainer directly
involved in a dispute should defer review to another uninvolved maintainer when
one is available. No guaranteed response time or confidential reporting service
is promised by this document.
